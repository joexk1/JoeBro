# Getting Started with JoeBro

Three steps to your first chat.

---

### Prerequisites

- macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later on Apple Silicon
- A model to talk to: Ollama (local), or an API key for DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Gemini, OpenRouter, etc.

---

### Step 1 — Launch

Open JoeBro. The local backend starts automatically on first run — no setup needed.

### Step 2 — Add a model

Click the **gear** (bottom-left of the sidebar) → **Add Model** → paste your API key or Ollama endpoint.

### Step 3 — Say something

Close Settings, type into the composer at the bottom of the window, and press Return.

---

### Nice-to-have extras

- **Theme it** — click the **◐** button in the sidebar footer to tune the glass effect, or set a wallpaper in Settings.
- **Connect email & calendar** — in Settings, add your IMAP and CalDAV accounts so the agent can work with them.
- **Bind a folder** — click **Bind to a folder** in the sidebar to give the agent a real project directory to work inside.

That's it. You're up and running with AI that lives on your machine, answers to you, and doesn't report back to anyone else. **This is what self-hosting should feel like — private by default, powerful when you need it, and yours to keep.**
